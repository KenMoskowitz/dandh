The Family Behind the Brand

D&H was founded in January 1959 by Arthur Duhamel and Harry Hawk — the first letters of their last names became the company's identity. Today, Arthur's grandson Brett Wright leads the team, carrying forward 65 years of family commitment to every home in Tucson. We're not a franchise. We're your neighbors.
