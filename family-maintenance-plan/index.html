Bi-Annual Tune-Ups

One tune-up every spring before the heat hits, and one in the fall before heating season. Your system stays in peak shape all year.

Priority Dispatch

When your AC goes out in a 110° Tucson summer, you go to the front of the line. Plan members get priority scheduling — no waiting behind non-members.

10% Off All Repairs

Parts and labor. Every repair, every time. When a capacitor or compressor needs replacing, you automatically save 10% off our standard rates.

Filter Replacement Included

Fresh filters during every tune-up visit. Clean filters mean better air quality, lower energy bills, and a longer-lasting system.

Full System Inspection

Our technicians inspect every major component — coils, refrigerant levels, electrical connections, drainage, and more. We catch small problems before they become expensive ones.

No After-Hours Surcharges

A Sunday night breakdown doesn't cost plan members extra. After-hours fees are waived for all Family Plan members — anytime, any day.

Warranty Compliance Maintained

Most manufacturers require documented annual maintenance to keep warranties valid. The Family Plan keeps your records clean and your coverage intact.
